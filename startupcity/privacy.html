Privacy Policy

Startup City · Last updated 8 November 2026

I’m Ilya Plotnikov, an independent developer. This policy explains what data Startup City handles and why. Startup City is a free idle game for Android. It has no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game sends gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics, so I can see where players get stuck. I don’t sell your data.
  • There are no ads and no advertising ID.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, your hero, the name you typed and your settings are saved on your phone. I never receive them. Android may include them in your Google account backup.

Game statistics

To see where players get stuck, how long they play and whether the game runs well on their phones, Startup City sends statistics to GameAnalytics, an analytics service for games. GameAnalytics receives:

  • what happens in the game: tutorial steps, jobs taken, goals reached, businesses opened, minutes played;
  • when sessions start and how long they last;
  • errors in the game and its frame rate;
  • your phone model, Android version, app version and connection type;
  • a random identifier the game creates and the Android ID;
  • your IP address, which GameAnalytics uses to find your country.

I see the statistics as reports in GameAnalytics and use them only to make the game better. GameAnalytics handles the data under its own privacy policy.

Reviews

Sometimes the game asks Google Play to show its rating window. Google Play shows it and handles your review. The game never learns whether you rated it.

Children

Startup City is not directed at children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

I don’t sell your data. The only third-party service in the game is GameAnalytics, as described above.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android and kept until you delete the game or its data. GameAnalytics receives the statistics over an encrypted connection and keeps them under its own policy. I keep no data about you myself and cannot link the statistics to you. If you want your statistics deleted, write to me and I will pass the request to GameAnalytics.

Changes

If the game starts using other services or collecting other data, I will update this page and the date at the top.
