Privacy Policy

Android games by Ilya Plotnikov · Last updated 27 September 2026

I’m Ilya Plotnikov, an independent developer. This policy explains what data my Android games handle and why. It covers every game listed under Games.

  • I have no servers and the games have no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The games show ads from Google AdMob. To show them, AdMob collects some device data.
  • If you allow it, a game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics. If you don’t, nothing is sent.

Data stored on your device

Each game saves your progress on your phone: levels, coins, settings and similar game data. I never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android may include it in your device backup to your Google Account, as it does for other apps; I have no access to that backup.

Advertising

The games show ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may receive:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language and game version;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

Google handles this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game statistics

To see which levels are too hard, where players stop and how the ads are used, a game can send statistics to GameAnalytics, an analytics service for games. It asks you first, the first time you open it, and sends nothing until you allow it. You can change your answer at any time in the game’s settings: Game statistics.

If you allow it, GameAnalytics receives:

  • what happens in the game: levels started, passed and given up, when you get stuck, the ads you ask for and watch, the coins you earn and spend;
  • identifiers of your device and the game: a random identifier the game creates, not linked to your name or accounts, and the identifiers Android gives apps — the advertising ID, if your device provides one, the app set ID and the Android ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version and game version;
  • your IP address, used to tell the country, and the time and length of your game sessions;
  • error reports when something in the game goes wrong.

I see the statistics in GameAnalytics as reports and use them only to make the games better. GameAnalytics says it never uses them to build advertising profiles, and handles the data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, each game asks for your consent to ads with Google’s consent form on first launch. Where US state privacy laws apply, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.
  • You can change your choice about ads at any time in the game’s settings: Ads and data. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they won’t be based on your interests.
  • You can turn the game statistics on or off at any time in the game’s settings: Game statistics.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path depends on your device).

Children

My games are not directed at children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

I don’t sell your data. The only third-party services in the games are Google AdMob and GameAnalytics, as described above.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android and kept until you delete the game or its data. Google AdMob and GameAnalytics receive data over encrypted connections and keep it under their own policies. To have the statistics sent from your device deleted, write to me at the address below.
