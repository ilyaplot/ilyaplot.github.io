Privacy Policy

Android games by Ilya Plotnikov · Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data the Android games by Ilya Plotnikov handle and why. It covers every game listed under Games. “We” means Ilya Plotnikov, the developer of these games.

  • We don’t collect your data and don’t keep it anywhere: we have no servers, and the games have no accounts.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The games show ads from Google AdMob. To show them, AdMob collects some device data.

Data stored on your device

Each game saves your progress on your phone: levels, coins, settings and similar game data. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android may include it in your device backup to your Google Account, as it does for other apps; we have no access to that backup.

Advertising

The games show ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may receive:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language and game version;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

Google handles this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, each game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form on first launch. Where US state privacy laws apply, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the game’s settings: Ads and data.
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but they won’t be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path depends on your device).

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell or share your data, because we don’t receive it. The only third-party service in the games is Google AdMob, as described above.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android and kept until you delete the game or its data. Google AdMob receives data over encrypted connections and keeps it under Google’s policy.

Games

  • AntColony — ads are rewarded only: you tap a button such as “Life for an ad” and get the reward after the video. There are no ads you didn’t ask for.

Changes

If a game starts using other services or collecting other data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
