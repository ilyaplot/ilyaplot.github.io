Privacy Policy

Glue.io · Last updated 21 November 2026

I’m Ilya Plotnikov, an independent developer. This policy explains what data Glue.io handles and why. Glue.io is a free casual game for Android. It plays offline and has no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game sends gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, so I can see where players get stuck. I don’t sell your data.
  • There are no ads and the game does not use the advertising ID.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, the balls you have unlocked, your rewards and your sound settings are saved on your phone. I never receive them. Android may include them in your Google account backup.

Game statistics

To see which maps are too hard, how long matches last and whether the game runs well on different phones, Glue.io uses Google Analytics for Firebase. When you are online, it receives:

  • what happens in the game: matches started and finished, places, missions completed, balls unlocked and upgraded;
  • when sessions start and how long they last;
  • your phone model, Android version, app version and language;
  • a random identifier of this installation of the game (the app instance ID);
  • your approximate location (country and region), which Google works out from your IP address.

The statistics do not include your name, email or contacts. I see them only as summary reports and use them to make the game better. Google handles the data under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Glue.io is not directed at children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

I don’t sell your data. The only third-party service in the game is Google Analytics for Firebase, as described above.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android and kept until you delete the game or clear its data. Google receives the statistics over an encrypted connection and keeps them for up to 14 months. Deleting the game also removes its identifier from your phone, so new statistics can no longer be linked to the old ones. If you want your statistics deleted, write to me.

Changes

If the game starts using other services or collecting other data, I will update this page and the date at the top.
