Delete your account

Total Tactics by Ilya Plotnikov · 6 November 2026

To delete your Total Tactics account, send an email to ilya.plotnikov.games@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you play with. Put “Delete my Total Tactics account” in the subject. You don’t need the game installed to do this.

I’ll reply to confirm and delete the account within 30 days. If I can’t find an account with your email address, I’ll ask for your player name.

What gets deleted

  • your account, with its Google account number and email address;
  • your fighters, items and currency, and the record of currency changes;
  • the battles and battle results linked to your account.

What stays

If you agreed to statistics, GameAnalytics has them, but they aren’t linked to your account, so I can’t find and delete yours. You can stop them at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy → Analytics.

If the battle server reported an error or suspected cheating during your battles, the report stays in Sentry until Sentry deletes it automatically, within 90 days.

You can also use the same address to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it. More about the data the game handles is in the privacy policy.
