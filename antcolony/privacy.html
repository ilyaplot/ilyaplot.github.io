Privacy Policy

AntColony for Android · Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data the AntColony game for Android handles and why. “We” means Ilya Plotnikov, the developer of AntColony.

  • We don’t collect your data and don’t keep it anywhere: we have no servers, and the game has no accounts.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. To show them, AdMob collects some device data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: your level, coins, win streak, lives, settings and an unfinished level. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android may include it in your device backup to your Google Account, as it does for other apps; we have no access to that backup.

Advertising

The game has rewarded ads only: you tap a button such as “Life for an ad”, Google AdMob shows a video, and the game gives you the reward. There are no ads you didn’t ask for.

To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may receive:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language and game version;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

Google handles this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form on first launch. Where US state privacy laws apply, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.
  • You can change your choice at any time: Settings on the main screen → Ads and data.
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but they won’t be based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path depends on your device).

Children

AntColony is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell or share your data, because we don’t receive it. The only third-party service in the game is Google AdMob, as described above.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by Android and kept until you delete the game or its data. Google AdMob receives data over encrypted connections and keeps it under Google’s policy.

Changes

If the game starts using other services or collecting other data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
